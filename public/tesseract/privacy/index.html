About this policy

This policy covers the Google OAuth connection used by Tesseract and its Hermes assistant, operated by Charlie Kalapati. The connection is for the owner's personal use with accounts the owner controls, including a dedicated assistant account. It is not a public service and does not accept public signups.

Google data accessed and why

The Google connection covers Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Gmail. The assistant accesses only data allowed by the permissions granted on Google's consent screen, and uses it to complete the owner's tasks. Depending on those permissions and the task, this can include:

  • Google Calendar: calendar details and event titles, descriptions, times, locations, and attendees, to look up and manage events and deadlines.
  • Google Drive: file names, metadata, and contents, to find, read, organize, and work with files.
  • Google Docs: document text and structure, to read, summarize, create, or edit documents.
  • Google Sheets: spreadsheet metadata, cell values, and formulas, to read, analyze, create, or update spreadsheets.
  • Gmail: message headers, senders, recipients, bodies, and attachments, to find, summarize, draft, send, or organize email.

The connection may also access account identification information to identify the connected account. The permissions shown by Google determine which data can be read, created, changed, or deleted.

Access tokens and refresh tokens allow the assistant to maintain that authorized connection. Google handles account sign-in; Tesseract does not receive the account's Google password.

Use and processing

Google data is used to perform the owner's requested assistant tasks and return their results. Relevant content from Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, or Gmail may pass through Hermes and intermediate task-processing steps before being sent to a hosted AI provider. That content may include excerpts, summaries, or original content needed to understand and complete the task.

The current AI provider is OpenAI. The owner may change the configured provider; this policy will be updated to identify the new provider before Google data is sent to it. AI providers may process and retain submitted content under their applicable service terms, privacy policies, and account settings, subject to the Google data-use restrictions described below.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train or improve generalized AI or machine learning models. Data is not shared for unrelated purposes. Apart from processing necessary for the requested task, disclosure is limited to what is required by law or necessary for security.

Storage and retention

Connection credentials are retained in the owner's privately operated assistant environment while the connection is in use. Task content and results may be retained in assistant history, logs, or files until the owner removes them. These public information pages do not store Google tokens or account contents.

The owner can disconnect the account and delete stored credentials and retained task data from the assistant environment. Revoking Google access stops future access, but does not automatically delete existing assistant history, copies retained by configured service providers, or items already saved in Google services.

Removing access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account connections. Select the Tesseract connection and remove its access. The owner can also delete data directly from the connected Google services.

For questions or a request to delete data held by the project, contact hi@charliekalapati.com.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Tesseract's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

These information pages

These pages do not use analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, or cookies. The website hosting provider may process standard request information, such as IP addresses and browser information, to deliver and secure the website.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if the connection's data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version. Any new use of Google data will require updating these disclosures and obtaining additional consent where required.
