What it does

Tesseract is a custom-built smart speaker that answers questions, plays music, and passes tasks to Hermes, a personal AI assistant. Its Google connection lets Hermes work with Google Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Gmail in an account controlled by the project owner. Depending on the permissions granted, tasks can include managing calendar events, finding and working with files, documents, and spreadsheets, and reading or sending email.

For personal use

This Google OAuth application is operated solely for the owner's personal use, including the Google account dedicated to Hermes. It is not offered as a public service, and there is no public signup or account connection on this website.

How the Google connection works

The owner authorizes access through Google's consent screen in the privately operated assistant. Access is limited to the permissions granted there and is used to carry out the owner's tasks. This page provides information about the connection; it does not collect Google credentials or initiate authorization.

Relevant Google content can pass through the assistant's processing steps to a hosted AI provider, currently OpenAI, to complete the owner's tasks. The configured provider may change. Read the privacy policy for information about Google data, storage, processing, and removing access.
